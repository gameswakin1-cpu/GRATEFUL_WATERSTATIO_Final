You are responsible for providing a complete delivery address and contact number and for being available to receive the delivery. Delivery timing may vary based on availability, location, and operating conditions.

Acceptable use

Changes and availability

Products and service features may change or become unavailable. We may update these draft terms as the service changes. Material updates should be communicated before they are applied to users.
