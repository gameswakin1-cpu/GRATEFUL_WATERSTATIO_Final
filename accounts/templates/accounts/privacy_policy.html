Information used

When you register or place an order, the service may use your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account details, order items, order status, and selected payment method. The service may also process technical information needed to keep your session and account working.

How information is used

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Receive, process, and coordinate orders and deliveries.
  • Contact you about an order, account, or service issue.
  • Maintain transaction records, protect the service, and meet applicable requirements.

When information may be shared

Information needed to fulfill an order may be available to authorized station staff and the assigned delivery rider. Information may also be handled by service providers supporting the website or disclosed when required by law. It should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis or notice.

Retention and protection

Account and order information should be retained only as long as needed for service, recordkeeping, and applicable requirements. The station should use reasonable safeguards to protect it; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact the station to ask about or request correction of your account information. Requests to delete information may be limited where records must be retained for an order or other applicable requirement.
